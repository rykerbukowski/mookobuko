## Photo inbox

Drop or copy your Photos app images into this folder.

They won't show up on the website automatically until a blog post references them.
Once you add images here, tell the assistant the post slug (e.g. `vietnam-food`) and any captions/order you want, and we’ll:

- move them into the right post folder under `public/media/posts/...`
- rename them to clean filenames
- reference them from the post markdown in `src/content/posts/<slug>.md`
