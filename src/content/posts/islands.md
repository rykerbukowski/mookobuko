## Getting There (Or: The Scam Heard Round the Ferry Terminal)

Right now it's "high season" in Thailand. I have never paid attention to that before, and I didn't think it would make much of a difference — maybe slightly more crowds? I've been to India. I can handle crowds. I was not worried.

What I failed to think about was the combination of **small island + large crowd = very little housing**.

Ryker and I took an overnight bus from Bangkok to a ferry terminal in **Chumphon**, arriving at 5am to wait for our ferry to Koh Tao — when we discovered the reasonably priced Airbnb we had booked was a scam.

If you guessed **prison**, you would be absolutely correct.

## The Whale Shark

On my final dive, mother nature herself looked down from the heavens and decided to grace me with her presence.

I was swimming back to the boat — goggles off — when my instructor started yelling to go back down. *"Back down?"* I thought. *"We just came up."* But he sounded so panicked I started to comply. My monkey brain immediately assumed a boat was about to run me over. In fact, in this very popular scuba spot with highly trained boatmen, this was not the case. In my tizzied state I lickety-split had my respirator in, goggles on, and turned to dive down.

The second my face went under — I went still.
